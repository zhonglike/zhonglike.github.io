05 /Anatomy — what actually travels

Small
packets.
Big idea.

Your message never travels as one piece. It is chopped into small packets, each labelled with where it belongs, so the network can route around trouble and the AI can put it back together in the right order.

Think of it like posting a letter in pieces — every envelope carries the address, a page number, and a seal. Lose one envelope and only that page is re-sent, never the whole letter.

01 · The wordsYOUR TEXT, AS NUMBERS
≈ 62% of the packet
02 · The addressWHERE IT IS GOING
≈ 12% of the packet
03 · The order no.WHICH PIECE IS WHICH
≈ 8% of the packet
04 · The sealENCRYPTION + CHECKSUM
≈ 18% of the packet
ONE PACKET, BY WEIGHT

Sizes are illustrative — real packets vary by network and app. The proportions are the point: most of the packet is your words; the rest is what gets them there safely.
